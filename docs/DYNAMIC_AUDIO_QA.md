The new `audio.placement(spec)` selects an action sound from an object's physical role and material: alternative canopies unfurl, air sources whoosh, metal lands with a metal sound, stone bounces and soft objects rustle. Existing random playback-rate variation and the eight-voice cap remain in effect. No new audio file, external service or download was added.

`tests/dynamic-audio-browser.mjs` drives real creation, placement, Undo, optional help, range controls, pause, resume and restart. Run it with `DYNAMIC_AUDIO_URL=http://127.0.0.1:4263 node tests/dynamic-audio-browser.mjs` against a built preview. It writes evidence to ignored `work/dynamic-audio/`.
