# Curriculum and evidence

Wordcraft Islands uses original physical puzzles to practise selected rules from the saved Workbook sources. This document is a mapping and evidence contract; a level win is not proof of retained mastery. Chapter 10 is outside this game's assessed scope. Speaking, pronunciation, live listening, extended writing, literature, the full tense system and full annual vocabulary bands are excluded.

## Evidence rules

A local attempt records the typed phrase, support use, retry, selected referent and resulting world action. A correct phrase after a shown answer, dictionary translation, accepted spelling suggestion or prior correction is assisted. Normalizing case/whitespace is not spelling help. An automatically supplied article is a model, never learner article evidence. A successful physical alternative remains a win even if it does not demonstrate the targeted English feature.

Unknown real words must receive a workshop-limit message, not a judgment that they are bad English. Only explicit reviewed errors (such as `a umbrellas`, `two crate`, or `an large umbrella`) receive precise grammar feedback. The finite parser is not a complete English grammar judge.

## Verification status

All nine authored challenges were completed through the browser UI by the independent reviewer, including the three changed-context transfer routes. Incorrect length, mass, referent, material and fan choices had recoverable world consequences. All 32 semantic tests pass, including supported full-phrase modeling, explicit endpoint support and narrow traversal width, assistance boundaries and duplicate/synonymous modifier evidence. Touch creation, placement, movement/cancellation and a ferry completion passed in portrait/landscape emulation. Evidence and exact commands are in PLAYTEST.md and docs/qa-captures.

Verification means these selected fixtures and observed interactions worked. It does not establish retained learning, all alternative language forms or full chapter coverage. Comparative-form production and relative-clause production are not assessed; identifying clauses guide actual referent selection, while additional information is taught without a separate comprehension score. The finite parser is a disclosed editor, not a general English grammar checker. Native device keyboard, real handheld performance and actual OS tab-focus behavior require device testing.

## Current play-feel scaffolds

The revision keeps the nine predicates/English missions. The first guided notebook in each grade automatically shows a model and records assistance. Existing-object edits prefill the old phrase; supplied article/count/noun spelling do not receive production credit. Independent and transfer models remain optional help. Snapped locations and automatic physical routes are interface support, not an English answer: target identity, length, coverage, mass, capacity, buoyancy and directional force still determine success. Movement skill is not assessed. Current evidence is in docs/UX_REVIEW.md and tests/ux*.
